Recipro - Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: February 19, 2026

Zero Tolerance Policy: Recipro has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content and activity on our platform.

Prevention Measures

  • Age Verification: Minimum age requirement of 18+ years for all users
  • Profile Verification: Mandatory profile verification during signup with tag-based discoverability requirements
  • Content Monitoring: Automated systems to detect and flag inappropriate content
  • User Reporting: In-app reporting system available on all profiles and chat conversations
  • Immediate Action: Instant account suspension and content removal for violations
  • Rating System: Community-driven feedback system to identify problematic users

Reporting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Users can report safety concerns through:

  • In-app report button available on any profile or chat
  • Direct email: nipunsharma@gmail.com

Our Response:

  • All reports reviewed within 24 hours
  • Immediate reporting to National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
  • Cooperation with local law enforcement authorities
  • Reporting to relevant regional authorities per jurisdiction
  • Permanent account termination for violators

Compliance

Recipro complies with:

  • All relevant child safety laws and regulations
  • Google Play's child safety standards policy
  • Regional and national reporting requirements
  • International standards for online child protection

Contact Information

Child Safety Inquiries: nipunsharma@gmail.com

Our designated contact is ready and able to discuss our child sexual abuse material (CSAM) prevention practices and compliance measures.

Continuous Improvement

We regularly review and update our safety measures to ensure the highest level of protection for all users. This policy is subject to updates as we enhance our safety systems and comply with evolving regulations.
