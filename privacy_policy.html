Privacy Policy for Recipro

Last Updated: February 18, 2026

Introduction

Mind Arena ("we", "our", or "us"), developer of Recipro, operates the Recipro mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Email Address: Used for account creation and authentication
  • Name: Your real name displayed in your profile
  • Age: Required for age verification (13-120 years)
  • Profile Photo: Optional photo you upload (JPEG, PNG, or WebP format, max 2MB)
  • Language Preference: Your selected language from 10 supported languages
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging Token: For push notifications

App Data

  • Tags and Interests: The 15+ tags you select describing your interests and expertise (10 common tags + 5 unique tags)
  • Points and Keys: Your current points balance, keys balance, and pending points
  • Ratings: Positive ratings, negative ratings, and total ratings count
  • Chat Messages: All messages you send and receive in one-on-one conversations
  • Chat History: Conversation participants, timestamps, read/unread status, typing indicators
  • Help Requests: Help requests you send and receive, including primary and secondary tags
  • Feedback: Ratings and feedback you give and receive
  • Conversation Status: Active, locked, or completed status of your chats
  • Ad Rewards: Timestamp of last ad reward earned

Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, session duration, search queries
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics for app performance and user behavior
  • Crash Reports: Error logs and stack traces to improve app stability (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Network Information: Connection status for offline/online functionality

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App functionality
  • Create and manage your user account
  • Enable search and discovery of helpers based on tags and interests
  • Facilitate one-on-one messaging between users
  • Send push notifications (new messages, help requests, feedback reminders)
  • Display your profile information to other users
  • Manage the points and keys reward system
  • Process ratings and feedback
  • Enforce the reciprocity system (locking/unlocking conversations)
  • Deliver rewarded advertisements
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Prevent fraud, spam, abuse, and inappropriate content
  • Enforce rate limits and fair usage policies
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

  • Firebase Services: Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers
  • Cloud Firestore: User profiles, tags, chats, and messages are stored in Firestore database
  • Firebase Storage: Profile photos are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage
  • Local Caching: Messages are cached locally on your device using Room database for offline access
  • Encryption: Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols
  • Access Control: Firestore security rules restrict data access - users can only read their own data and public profiles
  • Backend Security: Critical operations (profile creation, points/keys management, tag verification) are backend-only
  • Retention: Account data is retained until you delete your account

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Other Users: Your name, age, profile photo, tags, and ratings are visible to other users who search for help or chat with you
  • Chat Participants: Messages you send are visible to the recipient in one-on-one conversations
  • Service Providers: Firebase, AdMob, Google Play Services (as described above)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

Push Notifications

We send notifications for:

  • New messages in your conversations
  • New help requests received
  • Feedback reminders (48-hour deadline)
  • Rating requests from helpers
  • Conversation status changes (locked/unlocked)

You can enable or disable notifications:

  • Using the notification settings in the app
  • In your device settings under Recipro app permissions
  • Notifications are optional but recommended for the best experience
  • Disabling notifications does not affect your ability to use the app

Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies. However, third-party services (Firebase Analytics, AdMob) may use tracking technologies to collect usage data and serve personalized ads. You can manage ad personalization in your device settings.

Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Messages: Stored indefinitely unless you delete your account
  • Completed Chats: Marked as "completed" but not automatically deleted
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized data may be retained for analysis
  • Legal Compliance: Some data may be retained longer if required by law or for fraud prevention

International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, including the United States (Firebase servers). By using the App, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.

Data Deletion

  1. Contact us at infomindarena@gmail.com with your account deletion request

  3. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days
  4. Account deletion is irreversible and includes:
    • Profile information (name, age, photo, tags)
    • Points, keys, and ratings
    • All chat messages and conversation history
    • Help requests sent and received
    • Feedback given and received
  5. Some data may be retained for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution
  6. Anonymized analytics data may be retained
