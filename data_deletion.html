Account & Data Deletion Policy

Recipro by Mind Arena

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Recipro account and all associated data, please use the email request method:

Email Request

Email: infomindarena@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request - Recipro

Include in your email:

  • Your name or user ID (if known)
  • The email address associated with your account
  • Confirmation that you want to permanently delete your account

Email requests are processed within 30 days.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Permanently Deleted:

  • Account Information: Your Firebase authentication account and email address
  • Profile Information: Name, age, profile photo, and language preference
  • Tags and Interests: All 15+ tags you selected (common and unique tags)
  • Points and Keys: Your current points balance, keys balance, and pending points
  • Ratings: All positive ratings, negative ratings, and total ratings count
  • Chat Messages: All messages you sent and received in one-on-one conversations
  • Chat History: All conversation data, participants, timestamps, and read/unread status
  • Help Requests: All help requests you sent and received
  • Feedback: All ratings and feedback you gave and received
  • Device Tokens: FCM tokens used for push notifications
  • Active Conversations: You will be removed from any ongoing chats
  • Search History: Your search queries and preferences
  • Ad Rewards: Timestamp of last ad reward earned

Data That May Be Retained:

  • Messages in Other Users' Chats: Messages you sent may remain visible to other users as part of their chat history (your profile will be anonymized)
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized usage statistics may be retained for service improvement (cannot be linked back to you)
  • Legal Compliance: Certain data may be retained if required by law, fraud prevention, security purposes, or dispute resolution
  • Backup Data: Data in system backups may persist for up to 90 days before being permanently purged

Timeline

  • Email Requests: Processed within 30 days of receiving your request
  • Confirmation: You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted
  • Immediate Effect: Once deleted, you will be logged out and cannot access your account
  • Backup Purge: Data in system backups will be permanently purged within 90 days
⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted:
  • All your points and keys will be lost
  • Your ratings and reputation will be removed
  • All chat messages and conversation history will be deleted
  • You will be removed from any active conversations
  • Other users will no longer be able to find or contact you
  • Your profile photo will be deleted from Firebase Storage
  • You cannot recover your account or data
  • You will need to create a new account to use Recipro again

Alternative: Disable Notifications Only

If you only want to stop receiving notifications without deleting your account:

  1. Open the Recipro app
  2. Go to your profile or settings
  3. Toggle off notifications
  4. Or disable notifications in your device settings under Recipro app permissions

This keeps your account and progress intact while stopping all notifications.

Alternative: Take a Break

If you're not sure about permanent deletion, you can simply:

  • Stop using the app temporarily
  • Your account and data will remain intact
  • You can return anytime and continue where you left off
  • Your points, keys, ratings, and chat history will be preserved

What Happens to Your Conversations?

When you delete your account:

  • Active Chats: Other users will see that you are no longer available
  • Locked Conversations: Will be automatically unlocked for other users
  • Pending Feedback: Will be marked as unavailable
  • Your Messages: Will be removed from your side, but may remain visible to recipients (with your profile anonymized)

Data Deletion for Specific Information

If you only want to delete specific information without deleting your entire account:

  • Profile Photo: You can remove your photo in the app settings
  • Tags: You can update your tags in your profile
  • Name/Age: Contact us to request changes to your profile information

For partial data deletion requests, email us at infomindarena@gmail.com

Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Delete your data (as described above)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing
